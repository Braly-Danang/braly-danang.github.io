02

Face Data Collection, Use, and Storage

Some of our apps use face filter or sticker features that detect facial landmarks (such as the approximate position of your eyes, nose, mouth, and face outline) in real time while the camera is active, using on-device face detection technology provided by the mobile operating system (such as Apple's Vision/ARKit frameworks on iOS, or equivalent on-device face detection APIs on Android). This is used solely to align and render visual filters and stickers on your face in the live camera preview.

Collection. These apps do not collect, capture, or extract any biometric identifier or face template that could identify you. Facial landmark positions are detected transiently, frame-by-frame, only to position visual effects.

Processing location. All facial landmark detection is performed entirely on your device. No face data is transmitted to our servers or to any third party at any time.

Sharing. We do not share any face data with any third party, advertiser, analytics provider, or affiliate.

Storage and retention. Facial landmark data is not stored. It exists only in your device's memory while a single camera frame is being processed, and is discarded immediately afterward — typically within milliseconds. We do not retain, log, or database any face data, on-device or on our servers.

Deletion. Because no face data is stored anywhere, there is nothing to delete beyond the normal, immediate discarding described above.

Your saved photos/videos. If you choose to save a photo or video created using a filter, the resulting file (with the filter already applied) is saved only to your device's own photo library, as a direct result of your own action. This saved file is a standard photo/video and is not analyzed further, uploaded, or shared by us.
